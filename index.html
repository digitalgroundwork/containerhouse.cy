Our approach

A container is only a structure. A home is everything you decide to build inside it.

We design and build container homes and modular living spaces. Every project starts with the site, the way you live and the details that matter to you, then the steel shell is shaped to fit.

Layout, light, materials and finishes are decided together, so the finished home feels considered rather than assembled.
